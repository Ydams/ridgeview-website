Build Your Career at Ridgeview

From Academy internships to full-time roles on the Agency side — here's how to grow with us.

Why Work Here

Real Work, Real Growth

Ridgeview is small enough that your work is visible, and structured enough that your growth is intentional.

Real Client Work

No busywork — every role touches live agency projects or real learners from day one.

Fast Growth

We're early stage. That means more responsibility, faster, for people who show up and deliver.

Small, Sharp Team

You'll work directly with founders and senior staff, not get lost in layers of management.

Learn While You Work

Staff get access to Academy programs and internal knowledge-sharing sessions at no cost.

Our Pipeline

From Student to Staff

Most of our agency team started as Academy students. Here's the typical path.

1

Enroll

Join a Ridgeview program and complete your training with a real capstone project.

2

Stand Out

Top-performing students are identified during their final weeks of training.

3

Intern

Selected graduates join the Agency as paid interns, working on live client projects.

4

Join the Team

Strong interns are considered for full-time roles as the Agency scales.

Software Engineering Intern (Full Stack)

Frontend, backend and technology

Build and ship features on live client websites and Ridgeview's own products, working across the frontend and backend with senior developers reviewing your code.

Full role details

What you'll do

  • Build responsive interfaces with HTML, CSS, JavaScript and modern frameworks
  • Write and maintain APIs and database logic for client projects
  • Fix bugs, write tests and deploy with Git and GitHub
  • Take part in code reviews and weekly sprint planning

What we're looking for

  • Working knowledge of JavaScript and one backend stack (Node.js, Python or PHP)
  • Familiarity with Git and a database (SQL or NoSQL)
  • A portfolio or GitHub with projects you built yourself
  • Willingness to learn fast and take feedback
₦30k – ₦50k / monthLagos, hybrid

UI/UX Design Intern

Product design and user experience

Design interfaces and user flows for client websites, apps and Ridgeview's internal products, from early research through to handoff-ready Figma files.

Full role details

What you'll do

  • Turn briefs into wireframes, prototypes and high-fidelity screens in Figma
  • Run quick user research and usability tests, then act on the findings
  • Build and maintain design systems and component libraries
  • Work with developers so designs ship exactly as intended

What we're looking for

  • A portfolio that shows your process, not just final screens
  • Solid Figma skills and a good eye for layout, type and colour
  • Basic understanding of accessibility and responsive design
  • Clear communication when presenting design decisions
₦30k – ₦50k / monthLagos, hybrid
6-month paid internship

Digital Marketing Intern

Content, campaigns and analytics

Plan and run content and campaigns across Ridgeview's channels and for agency clients, then use the numbers to make each one better than the last.

Full role details

  • Strong writing and a feel for what works on each platform
  • Experience with Canva or similar design tools
