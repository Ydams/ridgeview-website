The story, structure, and people behind Ridgeview Innovations Hub — a technology-driven innovation and digital skills hub built for Nigeria's next generation of tech talent.

Our Story

Not Just a School. Not Just an Agency.

Ridgeview Innovations Hub Ltd was founded on a simple observation: Nigeria has no shortage of ambitious, talented people — what's often missing is a structured, affordable path from "I want to learn tech" to "I have a portfolio, a client, and an income."

So we built a dual model. On one side, the Digital Academy trains people in the practical skills employers and clients actually pay for. On the other, the Digital Agency delivers real IT solutions to real businesses. The two arms feed each other: academy graduates intern and work inside the agency, agency projects become the case studies and capstones academy students learn from, and every naira saved on staffing gets passed back into better training and more affordable pricing.

We're officially incorporated under the Companies and Allied Matters Act 2020, based in Lagos, and building toward a pan-African footprint over the next three years.

What We Stand For

Our Core Values

The principles that shape how we teach, build, and work with every learner, client, and partner.

Practicality

We teach and build for the real world, not the theoretical one. Every module ends in something you can show, not just something you know.

Integrity

We're a registered, accountable institution. What we promise in a program outline is what happens in the classroom.

Community

Learning is easier and more sustainable in a community. Every cohort, bootcamp, and event is designed to build lasting peer networks.

Access

Flexible pricing, payment plans, and virtual options exist so that ability, not affordability, determines who gets to learn.

Innovation

We stay close to what employers and clients actually need today, and adjust our curriculum and services accordingly.

Excellence

70% hands-on, 30% theory, with real capstones — because a certificate should mean something when it lands on a hiring manager's desk.
