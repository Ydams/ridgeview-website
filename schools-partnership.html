Schools Partnership

Bring Digital Skills Training Into Your School

Ridgeview partners with secondary schools, universities, and polytechnics to deliver structured digital skills programs — from single workshops to full curriculum integration.

Why Partner With Us

Give Your Students a Real Head Start

Employers increasingly expect graduates to arrive with practical digital skills. We help institutions close that gap without building a tech curriculum from scratch.

Job-Ready Curriculum

Our program content is built around the skills employers and clients actually pay for today, not outdated syllabi.

Hands-On Delivery

70% practical, 30% theory. Students leave every session having built or shipped something real.

Certified Outcomes

Every participant receives a Ridgeview certificate on completion, backed by a CAC-registered institution.

Internship Pathway

Standout students can be considered for paid internships within the Ridgeview Digital Agency.

Institutional Pricing

Bulk enrollment and school-wide packages are priced well below our standard individual program rates.

Dedicated Support

A single point of contact manages scheduling, facilitator logistics, and reporting for your institution.

Partnership Options

Choose the Right Fit for Your Institution

From a one-off workshop to a full-term embedded program — every tier is customised after an initial consultation.

Workshop Series

A short, focused introduction — ideal for career weeks, clubs, or taster sessions.

  • 1–4 week workshop series
  • One program track of your choice
  • Certificate of participation
  • On-campus or virtual delivery
Most Chosen

Term Program

A full-term structured program, run alongside the academic calendar.

  • One full academic term (10–14 weeks)
  • Choice of up to 3 program tracks
  • Capstone projects & Ridgeview certification
  • Progress reporting to school administration

Full Integration

Digital skills embedded into the institution's curriculum year-round.

  • Multi-term, recurring cohorts
  • Full program catalogue access
  • Priority consideration for agency internships
  • Dedicated account manager
